# Microsoft Form: Training Registration

| # | Question | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name | Text | Yes |
| 2 | Email | Text (restrict to email format) | Yes |
| 3 | Department | Text or Choice | Yes |
| 4 | Session | Choice | Yes |

**Critical rule:** each choice in Q4 must be identical (spelling, case, spacing) to a `Title` in the Sessions list, because Flow A matches on it.

Settings: restrict to people in your organisation; record name = off if you collect Name as a question.

Maintenance tip: when you add a Session, add the same text as a new choice in Q4 (see SOP section 3).

## Feedback Form (used by Flow C)
1. Overall rating (1-5)
2. Trainer effectiveness (1-5)
3. Content relevance (1-5)
4. What should we improve? (text)
